About Authr

Authr is a private two-factor authenticator. It generates 6-digit codes on this device for your accounts.

Your secrets never leave this browser. Codes are generated on-device and nothing is sent to any server.

If you clear this site's data, your accounts are gone. Export a backup from the menu first.
